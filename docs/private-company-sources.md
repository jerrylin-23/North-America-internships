# Private company additions

Checked on 2026-09-20. These additions use a US$1 billion total funding threshold, not a valuation threshold. The amounts below establish the threshold; they are not complete current funding totals. Company sizes vary. No fixed employee-count limit was applied.

All listed job boards returned job data through a supported provider. A working board does not mean it currently has a matching internship. The existing Canada and US role filters still apply.

- **SambaNova:** The company reported more than US$1 billion in total funding in its [April 2021 announcement](https://sambanova.ai/press/series-d). [Forge confirms private status](https://forgeglobal.com/sambanova-systems_ipo/).
- **PsiQuantum:** Its [September 2025 Series E alone raised US$1 billion](https://www.psiquantum.com/news-import/psiquantum-1b-fundraise). [Nasdaq Private Market confirms private status](https://www.nasdaqprivatemarket.com/company/psiquantum/).
- **Relativity Space:** Its [US$500 million Series D](https://www.relativityspace.com/press-release/2020/11/23/relativity-space-closes-500m-series-d-financing-as-it-solidifies-sector-leading-momentum) and [US$650 million Series E](https://www.relativityspace.com/press-release/2021/6/08/relativity-space-fundraise-series-e) establish at least US$1.15 billion across those two rounds. [Nasdaq Private Market confirms private status](https://www.nasdaqprivatemarket.com/company/relativity-space/).
- **Zipline:** [Forbes reported more than US$900 million raised by April 2023](https://www.forbes.com/sites/alexkonrad/2023/04/28/drone-delivery-startup-zipline-boosts-valuation-to-4-billion/). Zipline then announced [more than US$600 million in January 2026](https://www.zipline.com/newsroom/zipline-surpasses-2-million-deliveries-raises-more-than-600m-to-power-next-phase-of-growth-and-expands-operations-to-houston-and-phoenix). These reports establish more than US$1.5 billion. [Nasdaq Private Market confirms private status](https://www.nasdaqprivatemarket.com/company/zipline/).
- **Reflection AI:** The company [announced US$2 billion raised in October 2025](https://reflection.ai/blog/frontier-open-intelligence). [Nasdaq Private Market lists it as a private company](https://www.nasdaqprivatemarket.com/company/reflection-ai/).
- **Figure AI:** Lead investor Parkway states that the [2025 Series C closed above US$1 billion](https://www.parkway.vc/portfolio/figure). This confirms closing beyond Figure's initial committed-capital announcement. [Forge confirms private status](https://forgeglobal.com/figure-ai_ipo/). This is the robotics company, not the public financial-services company Figure Technology Solutions.
- **Sierra:** The company announced [US$350 million raised in September 2025](https://sierra.ai/uk/blog/theres-an-agent-for-that-and-it-runs-on-sierra), followed by [US$950 million in May 2026](https://sierra.ai/blog/better-customer-experiences-built-on-sierra). [Axios reported the latter round as raised](https://www.axios.com/pro/enterprise-software-deals/2026/05/04/sierra-ai-valuation-agentic-enterprise). These rounds total US$1.3 billion. [Nasdaq Private Market confirms private status](https://www.nasdaqprivatemarket.com/company/sierra/).
- **World Labs:** The company [reported US$1 billion in new funding in February 2026](https://www.worldlabs.ai/blog/funding-2026). [Nasdaq Private Market confirms private status](https://www.nasdaqprivatemarket.com/company/world-labs/).
- **Airwallex:** The company [reported more than US$1.2 billion in total funding in May 2025](https://www.airwallex.com/newsroom/airwallex-raises-usd300-million-at-a-usd6-2-billion-valuation-to-build-the). That announcement includes US$150 million in secondary transfers; subtracting those still leaves more than US$1.05 billion. [Forge confirms private status](https://forgeglobal.com/airwallex_ipo/).
- **Shield AI:** The company confirmed that its [US$2 billion financing package closed in June 2026](https://shield.ai/shield-ai-completes-acquisition-of-aechelon-technology/). The package includes US$1.5 billion in Series G funding and US$500 million in preferred equity. [Forge confirms private status](https://forgeglobal.com/shield-ai_ipo/).

- **Neuralink:** Nasdaq Private Market records US$600 million in Series E, US$280 million in Series D, and US$206 million in Series C, which alone total US$1.086 billion. Its [company profile also confirms private status](https://www.nasdaqprivatemarket.com/company/neuralink/). These conservative figures exclude other recorded rounds.
- **Redwood Materials:** The company [closed more than US$1 billion in Series D equity in 2023](https://www.redwoodmaterials.com/news/redwood-series-d/). This excludes its separate government loan commitment. [Nasdaq Private Market confirms private status](https://www.nasdaqprivatemarket.com/company/redwood-materials/).
- **Physical Intelligence:** [Reuters reported US$400 million raised in November 2024](https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/robot-ai-startup-physical-intelligence-raises-400-million-from-bezos-openai-3700151). [Nasdaq Private Market confirms private status](https://www.nasdaqprivatemarket.com/company/physical-intelligence/).
- **Helion:** Its [company FAQ, updated May 2026](https://www.helionenergy.com/faq), confirms more than US$1 billion raised in private capital and states that the company is not publicly traded.
- **Lambda:** The company [reported more than US$1.5 billion raised in Series E in November 2025](https://lambda.ai/blog/lambda-raises-over-1.5b-from-twg-global-usit-to-build-superintelligence-cloud-infrastructure). This equity round alone meets the limit; credit facilities are not required. [Nasdaq Private Market confirms private status](https://www.nasdaqprivatemarket.com/company/lambda/).
- **Form Energy:** [Associated Press reported more than US$1.2 billion raised from investors in October 2024](https://apnews.com/article/822f329c8a4f7518f4db6b24d6fcd15c). This earlier closed round meets the limit without counting the anticipated full amount of its 2026 Series F. [Forge confirms private status](https://forgeglobal.com/crusoe-energy-systems_ipo/).
- **Commonwealth Fusion Systems:** The company [reported US$4 billion in total capital raised in July 2026](https://www.cfs.energy/news-and-media/commonwealth-fusion-systems-raises-another-1-billion-bringing-total-capital-raised-to-4-billion/). Its [May 2026 partnership announcement identifies it as a private company](https://www.cfs.energy/news-and-media/astar-and-commonwealth-fusion-systems-sign-a-collaborative-research-agreement-to-advance-fusion-supply-chain-capabilities-in-singapore/).
- **Saronic:** The company [closed US$1.75 billion in Series D funding](https://medium.com/saronic-technologies/saronic-closes-1-75b-series-d-at-9-25b-valuation-to-accelerate-a-new-era-of-maritime-autonomy-a801be818746). Funding totals and private status can change. Recheck the sources before using this list for a later funding-based selection.
